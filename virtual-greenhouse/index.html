Photo sources and scale

Each clear-pot view combines a photographed shoot with a root scan from a separate Brassica rapa specimen. The shoots represent stages near the lab sampling days. The roots show an early-to-late scan sequence. The ruler is calibrated to the simulated shoot height.

Shoots are cropped from Wisconsin Fast Plants® Life Cycle Time Lapse by the Wisconsin Fast Plants® Program. The source frames show plants at about 3, 7, 13 and 20 days after sowing. These adapted shoot cutouts are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Roots are cropped and recolored from Figure 1C of Adu et al. (2014), Journal of Experimental Botany, licensed CC BY 3.0.
